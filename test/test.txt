## Test Suite Coverage

### 1. `streakEngine.test.js` (15 tests)
- **Happy Path**:
  - Contiguous date range generation (`getDateRange`).
  - Formatting ISO date strings and objects (`formatDate`, `getTodayString`).
  - Dual tracking philosophy resolution (`getHabitDayStatus` for `skip` and `complete` types).
  - Metrics computation (`currentStreak`, `longestStreak`, `completionRate`, `streakStartDate`).
  - Tiered milestone progress and unlocked badges (`calculateTierProgress`).
  - Monthly calendar matrix calculation with weekday alignment (`generateMonthCalendar`).
  - Aggregated multi-habit dashboard summary (`calculateAllHabitsSummary`).
  - 7-day weekly repeatingness frequency (`calculateWeeklyFrequency`).
- **Edge Cases**:
  - Inverted date ranges (`start > end`).
  - Single-day start/end boundaries.
  - Zero tracked days and future start dates.
  - Backdated completions occurring before habit creation.
  - Full milestone unlocks when streaks exceed 5 years.
- **Error Handling**:
  - Invalid date parsing (NaN/non-date strings).
  - Missing or null habit objects handled without unhandled exceptions.
